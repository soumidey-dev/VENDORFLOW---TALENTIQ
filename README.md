# 🚀 VendorFlow — TalentIQ

### Intelligent Business & Career Automation Platform

VendorFlow — TalentIQ combines two AI-powered applications:

- 💼 **VendorFlow Finance** — Autonomous B2B Invoice Audit, Fraud Check & Payout Engine
- 🎓 **CareerMatch Intelligence** — AI-Powered Career & Placement Intelligence

> ⚠️ Hackathon Demo — No Real Money Transfer

---

## 💼 VendorFlow Finance

An AI-powered invoice automation system that helps businesses analyze invoices, verify purchase orders, detect fraud, calculate risk, and automate low-risk financial workflows.

### Key Features

- 📄 AI Invoice & Document Analysis
- 🔍 Purchase Order Reconciliation
- 🛡️ Fraud & Anomaly Detection
- 📊 Deterministic Risk Scoring
- 🤖 Autonomous Approval Workflow
- 👨‍💼 Human-in-the-Loop Review
- 💳 Simulated Payment Processing
- 🧾 Digital Payment Receipts
- 🔐 SHA-256 Audit Trail
- 🏢 Vendor & Finance Admin Portals

### Workflow

```text
Invoice Upload
      ↓
Gemini AI Extraction
      ↓
PO & Vendor Verification
      ↓
Fraud / Risk Analysis
      ↓
Auto-Approve OR Human Review
      ↓
Simulated Payment
      ↓
Receipt + Audit Trail
